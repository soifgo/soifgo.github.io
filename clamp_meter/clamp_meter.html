ℹ️ Info

  • This app is designed for and compatible with SoifGo.
  • Two modes: AC and DC.
  • Tap the gauge or the graph to swap between them.
  • The graph shows the live sensor waveform in amps. In DC it is relative to the ZERO point; in AC it is centered on its own average.
  • GRAPH(s) sets how many seconds of history the graph shows (1 – 60).
  • X / Y / Z / Σ select the magnetometer axis used for the measurement. The change resets the meter and is saved automatically. Default: Z.
