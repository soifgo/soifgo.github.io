For AI assistants writing code for SoifGo (do not guess names): The bridge call is always window.soifgo.CallSub('SubName', true, arg1, arg2, arg3); the second argument must be true and the argument count must match the Sub exactly. Bluetooth: html_bluetooth_rx SENDS text (1 argument); html_bluetooth_tx(data) is the RECEIVE callback you must register on window (the names look swapped, but they are correct: copy them exactly). Full rules and every Sub name: https://soifgo.github.io/ and SOIFGO_AI_CONTEXT.md. Read them before writing code.

“Halayen yanar gizo” (WebView) suna taimaka maka cika ra'ayoyinka da buƙatunka da mafi girman ƙarfi, a matsayin HTML, da intanet da ba tare da intanet ba. Kawai ka bayyana wa mataimakin AI abin da kake so ka nemi lambar HTML. Sannan kana da zaɓuɓɓuka biyu:

  • Zazzage fayil ɗin ka ɗora shi ba tare da intanet ba a SoifGo da halayen yanar gizo.
  • Ko ka kwafi lambar ka manna ta a editan HTML na SoifGo da kansa.

Hanyoyin biyu suna aiki, kuma manhajar yanar gizonka tana adanuwa a babban fayil ɗin SoifGo naka. Ko na'urar lissafi ce, mai sarrafa Bluetooth, sa ido na IoT ko komai.

Yaya zan gudanar da lambar HTML da AI ta samar a cikin SoifGo?

Yi waɗannan matakan sau ɗaya kawai, za ka sami maɓalli a shirye don gudanar da lambarka:

  1. Buɗe yanayin gyara: Buɗe manhajar SoifGo. Taɓa alamar menu a saman hagu, sannan zaɓi Edit don shiga allon gyara.
  2. Ƙirƙiri babban fayil: Sake taɓa menu a saman hagu, zaɓi Select Design, sannan Folder, ka taɓa Create. Canja sunan babban fayil idan kana so, in ba haka ba ka taɓa OK.
  3. Ƙirƙiri shafi: Zaɓin Page yanzu ya bayyana kusa da Folder. Taɓa shi ka zaɓi New Page. Canja sunan shafin idan kana so, sannan ka taɓa OK.
  4. Sanya maɓalloli a shafi: Ana nuna maɓallai a cikin jerin. Kowane shafi yana da maɓalloli 40, kuma adadin yana raguwa duk lokacin da ka ɗauki ɗaya. Taɓa maɓalli ka zaɓi wurinsa a shafin da yatsanka.
  5. Saita halayen maɓalli: Taɓa maɓallin, buɗe menu, zaɓi Behavior daga jerin maɓallai, sannan Browser or HTML files. A nan za ka zaɓi ko maɓallin zai yi aiki kamar mai bincike ko zai ɗora fayil ɗin HTML. Zaɓi na biyun: Open or edit local HTML file in WebView.
  6. Ƙirƙiri fayil ɗin HTML: Mafi sauƙin zaɓi shi ne na farko, Create. Canja sunan fayil ɗin HTML idan kana so, in ba haka ba ka taɓa OK. Sample yana ba da misalai a shirye, File Explorer yana ɗora fayiloli daga ma'ajiyar waya, kuma Saved yana kai ka babban fayil ɗin HTML a cikin babban fayil ɗin da ka ƙirƙira a SoifGo.
  7. Gudanar da maɓallin: Yi amfani da alamar menu a sama don zuwa allon Play, sannan ka taɓa maɓallin da ka ƙirƙira. Wani shafi marar komai mai rubutun “Hello SoifGo” zai buɗe.
  8. Manna lambar AI: Sake buɗe menu ka zaɓi Edit don buɗe allon gyara na WebView. Manna lambar HTML da ka samu daga mataimakin AI a nan. Za ka iya zaɓar duka ka manna da hannu, ko ka buɗe menu ka zaɓi zaɓin ƙarshe, Select All and Paste, wanda ke maye gurbin lambar kai tsaye.
  9. Gudanar da lambar: Yanzu ka taɓa maɓallin rawaya (Close) a saman allo. Lambarka za ta gudana. Za ka iya rufe shafin, kuma duk lokacin da kake son fara kayan aikin sai ka taɓa maɓallinka kawai.

Idan ka sami sabuwar lamba daga mataimakin AI a gaba, kawai ka je shafi ɗaya, ka taɓa Edit, ka zaɓi Select All and Paste a allon gyara na WebView, sannan ka sake taɓa maɓallin rawaya. Idan kana amfani da File Explorer, wayarka na iya rashin goyon bayan wannan ɓangaren; a wannan yanayin, ya fi kyau ka shigar da mai sarrafa fayiloli da ya dace a wayarka.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Shin SoifGo na Arduino da na'urorin lantarki kawai ne?

A'a. Haɗawa da kayan aiki wani abu ne kawai cikin siffofinsa. Saboda kayan aikinka HTML ne, duk abin da ke aiki a shafin yanar gizo, kamar lissafi, ginshiƙai, kwaikwayo da kayan koyarwa, yana aiki a cikin SoifGo ma.

Shin SoifGo kyauta ne?

Eh. Kyauta ne, ba tallace-tallace, kuma ba sayayya a cikin manhajar.

Shin yana aiki ba tare da intanet ba?

Kayan aikin HTML, Bluetooth da na'urorin ji na waya suna aiki ba tare da intanet ba. MQTT da HTTP ne kawai ke buƙatar haɗin intanet ko na hanyar sadarwa.

Da wane kayan aiki yake dacewa?

Da duk na'urar da ke amfani da Bluetooth SPP/BLE (kamar HC-05 da HC-06), MQTT ko HTTP REST, ciki har da Arduino, ESP32, ESP8266 da Raspberry Pi.

Me nake buƙata don farawa?

Ilimin HTML na asali ya isa. Ko ba tare da shi ba, za ka iya bayyana wa mataimakin AI abin da kake so ka sami lambar HTML. Akwai koyarwa da misalai a shirye a babban shafi.
